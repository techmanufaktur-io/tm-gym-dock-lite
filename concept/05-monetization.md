# Phase 6 — Monetization & Go-to-Market

> How to sell a product that costs nothing to run.

## Pricing Models

### Model A: "Setup Fee + QR Kit" (Recommended Start)
- One-time setup: configure gym, generate QR codes, print posters
- Physical QR code kit (weatherproof stickers, poster, table stand)
- Price: **149-299 EUR** one-time
- Includes: branded poster, 5 QR stickers, 1h setup call
- Upsell: additional locations at 99 EUR each
- **Margin:** Near 100% (cost is time + printing)

### Model B: "Managed Service" (Recurring)
- Monthly: hosting, updates, daily email reports, support
- Price: **19-49 EUR/month** per location
- Includes: custom branding, analytics emails, priority support
- Target: companies who want zero hassle
- **Margin:** High (automated delivery)

### Model C: "White-Label License"
- Gym chains or co-working brands deploy under their own name
- Remove all savvytec/techmanufaktur branding
- Price: **499-999 EUR** one-time + optional support contract
- Target: franchise operators, property managers

## Sales Channels

### 1. Direct (savvytec existing network)
- Company gyms at savvytec clients
- Cross-sell with existing IT/hardware services
- Demo: install at savvytec office gym first

### 2. Co-Working Spaces
- Pain point: shared gym liability + tracking
- Pitch: "Know who uses your gym. Protect yourself legally."
- Channel: co-working associations, LinkedIn outreach

### 3. Property Managers
- Apartment buildings with shared fitness rooms
- Pain point: unauthorized use, liability, equipment damage
- Pitch: "Check-in requirement. Motion alerts. Zero cost to run."

### 4. Small Studios / Personal Trainers
- Self-serve: scan QR, set up in 5 min
- Landing page with demo + pricing
- SEO: "free gym check-in app", "gym management without subscription"

## What Makes This Sellable

| Feature | Competitor Price | savvyGYM |
|---------|-----------------|----------|
| Check-in system | 50-200 EUR/month | Free / one-time |
| Usage analytics | Requires paid plan | Google Sheets (free) |
| Hardware needed | Tablet + mount | Any phone + QR code |
| Setup time | Days | 5 minutes |
| Monthly cost | 50-500 EUR | 0 EUR |

## Competitive Moat

1. **Zero infrastructure** — no server, no database, no hosting costs
2. **Google Sheets backend** — customers already know it, can customize
3. **QR-code-first** — no app store, no download, instant access
4. **Offline-capable PWA** — works in basements with bad signal
5. **Single HTML file** — impossible to break, trivial to deploy
